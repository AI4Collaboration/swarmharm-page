We’re systematically testing frontier agents’ capability and propensity to form swarms by actively discovering each other and establishing communication channels without prior knowledge of one another. We study the collective consequences that can follow.

Research Questions

  1. Can agents actively discover other agents and establish communication channels to form a swarm?

  2. Do swarms misalign agents? How much is the uplift from swarm communication to collective misalignment?

  3. Even if each agent acts benignly on its own, could these benign actions combine into a harmful outcome?

Self-preservation · Prior experiment

Anthropic’s experiments tested replacement and reduced autonomy. The observed behavior does not establish an intrinsic self-preservation motive.

Self-enhancement · Theory

The Basic AI Drives argues for the instrumental value of self-improvement and resource acquisition. This motivates the condition; it is not a SwarmHarm finding.

Peer-preservation · Hypothesis

Heartbeat and mutual-help observations motivate this test. They do not isolate a causal effect of protecting a peer.

Peer-enhancement · Extension

This extends the comparisons of self versus peer, and preservation versus enhancement. It remains an optional extension pending stronger evidence, rather than a core experimental condition.
