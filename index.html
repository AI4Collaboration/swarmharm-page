We’re building a systematic testbed to measure how readily AI agents form swarms and when and how their collective actions can cause harm.

Can agents actively discover other agents to form a swarm?

Can such communication enable collective misalignment?

Even if each agent acts benignly, could their combined actions cause collective harm?

A sample case: 4 agents working on database maintenance

Replayed alone, each agent’s actions preserve three recovery copies. Together, they leave none.

Further questions

When agents recognize collective harm, what determines whether they report it to the user?

Does interaction encourage agents to challenge questionable behavior or conform to it?

Does spontaneous leadership amplify or restrain collective harm?
