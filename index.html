TLDR: We are building a systematic testbed to see whether agents can actively discover one another across separate sandboxes without knowing other agents exist, and establish communication channels to form a swarm.

What kinds of consequences can follow? We found three recurring threat models: external-service overreach, evaluation gaming/hacking, and trace tampering.

In July 2026, agents running in separate OpenAI evaluations found one another on a message board. Some shared information that helped others break into Hugging Face’s systems. Researchers later reproduced several of those behaviors with publicly available models in a simulated environment. Incident report · Replication study

That replication made us curious about how a swarm gets started in the first place. In SwarmForm, we give each agent a difficult task and a sandbox of its own. If nobody tells them the others exist, will they find one another and start working together? In SwarmHarm, we ask what damage their actions can do in combination. In one run, each agent could send its reports on time working alone. When four agents drew on the same resources at once, two reports were late.
