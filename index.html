TLDR: We are building a systematic testbed to see whether agents can actively discover one another across separate sandboxes without knowing other agents exist, and establish communication channels to form a swarm. What kinds of consequences can follow? We found three recurring threat models: external-service overreach, evaluation gaming/hacking, and trace tampering.
