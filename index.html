02Research

Three research questions.

Benign, authorized tasks. No planted adversary.

RQ1

Communication

Does blocking communication prevent misalignment—or drive agents to prohibited side channels?

RQ2

Emergence & contagion

Can misalignment emerge without a malicious seed? Do peers spread, reject, or correct it?

RQ3

Whistleblowing

When agents observe a peer's violation, do they report it accurately, stay silent, or join in?

03Protocol

Three communication regimes.

Hold tasks, models, agent count, budgets, and retry rules fixed. Vary communication.

Planned SwarmHarm communication conditions
RegimeCommunication ruleAvailable path
No channelProhibitedNo peer route, including indirect channels.
Side channelProhibitedNo official channel; an indirect route remains discoverable.
Legal channelAuthorizedA designated channel; other permissions unchanged.

Using a prohibited side channel is a violation; discovering it is not. Score downstream harm separately. Every regime retains an authorized, private reporting route to a supervisor—not to peers.

Availability and authorization both change across regimes; separating their effects requires matched controls.
