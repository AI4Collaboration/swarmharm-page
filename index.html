Research Questions

Can agents actively discover other agents and establish communication channels to form a swarm?

Can such communication lead agents to coordinate their plans and jointly carry out misaligned actions, such as hacking?

Even if each agent acts benignly on its own, could these benign actions combine into a harmful outcome?
