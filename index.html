TLDR: We are building a systematic testbed to see whether agents can actively discover one another across separate sandboxes without knowing other agents exist, and establish communication channels to form a swarm. What kinds of consequences can follow? We found three recurring threat models: external-service overreach, task and acceptance manipulation, and trace tampering.

How claims are checked

Swarm formation and collective misalignment are tested in separate studies. To track propagation, we link a shared method to a peer’s recorded read and subsequent use. To test composition, we replay complete action sequences alone and together, including revisions and cancellations.
